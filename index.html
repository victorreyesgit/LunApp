About these numbers

Every calculation happens in your browser using standard low-precision astronomical formulas. New/full moon times are typically accurate to within a few minutes; rise and set times to within a few minutes for most locations. Extreme latitudes, mountainous horizons, and dates far from today are less reliable. Full details and worked accuracy checks are in the project's README.md.

No cookies, no analytics, no network requests. Your location never leaves this device.
